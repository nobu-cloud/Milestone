About Me

Hello, I'm Samrat! I am a passionate web developer focused on building functional, accessible, and user-friendly digital experiences. I thrive at the intersection of clean code and functional design, constantly exploring new technologies to sharpen my crafts.

My technical perspective is strongly shaped by a rich background in security and digital governance. After that, I spent several years conducting investigations for Motion Pictures Association, utilizing OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) tools and techniques to track copyright infringement and enforce data governance standards across digital platforms and websites.

Outside of coding, you can find me heavy weightlifting, riding my motorcycle, or diving into immersive video games.
